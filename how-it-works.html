How Hunny Invests Works

From deposit to payout — here's exactly what happens at every stage.

1

Deposit into your wallet

Create an account, then request a deposit. We'll show you payment instructions — send funds and share your reference with our admin team. Once verified, your available wallet balance increases.

2

Browse opportunities

Each opportunity shows the product, required capital, funding progress, advertised return, duration, and minimum/maximum investment — before you commit anything.

3

Invest from your wallet

Enter an amount, preview your expected return and maturity payout, and confirm. The amount moves from your available balance to a locked investment balance.

4

Funding reaches 100%

Once an opportunity is fully funded, the owner is notified and can purchase the required stock — this must happen within the opportunity's stock purchase window.

5

Stock purchased — timer starts

When the owner confirms the stock purchase, the investment period officially begins. Your maturity date is set from this point, not from when you invested.

6

Sales are tracked

The owner manually records sales and remaining stock throughout the investment period. You can follow progress from your investment detail page.

7

Maturity & settlement

At maturity: if at least one unit sold, you receive your principal plus the advertised return. If zero units sold, you receive your principal minus a 1% fee.

8

Withdraw anytime

Once funds are back in your available balance, request a withdrawal. Our admin team verifies and processes it, typically within 24 hours.

What if the owner never purchases stock?

If the stock purchase deadline passes without the owner buying stock, every investor is refunded in full — with no early-exit fee.

Can I exit early?

Yes — but only before the investment period starts (i.e. before the owner purchases stock). A 2% fee applies. Once the timer starts, funds are locked until maturity.
