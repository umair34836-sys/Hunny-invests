About Hunny Invests

Hunny Invests is a marketplace that connects everyday investors with small, real-world business opportunities — starting with small-batch product ventures such as apparel and retail stock.

Our Mission

We believe investing shouldn't require large capital or financial jargon. Our platform lets anyone start with a small amount, understand exactly what they're funding, and see a clear, fixed return calculation before they commit a single rupee.

How Opportunities Work

Verified business owners list an opportunity describing the product, the capital required, the stock quantity, and the return they're offering investors. Once an opportunity is 100% funded, the owner purchases stock and starts the investment timer. At maturity, investors receive their principal plus the advertised return (if at least one unit sold) or their principal minus a small fee (if nothing sold).

Our Commitment to Transparency

  • Every opportunity clearly separates investor return from actual business profit.
  • Funding can never exceed 100% of the stated target.
  • Deposits and withdrawals are manually verified by our admin team — no funds move without a human check.
  • Every sensitive action on the platform is recorded in an audit log.

Who Can Use Hunny Invests

Investors deposit funds and invest in opportunities. Owners (approved manually by our admin team) create and manage opportunities. Admins oversee the entire platform, verify deposits/withdrawals, and resolve disputes.

This is a product-stage platform. Before accepting public real-money investment at scale, we are committed to obtaining Pakistan-specific legal and compliance guidance. Please read our Risk Disclosure.
