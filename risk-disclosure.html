Risk Disclosure

Investing through Hunny Invests carries real financial risk, including the risk of not receiving the advertised return. Please read this page in full before investing.

1. Not a Guaranteed Return

The advertised investor return on any opportunity is conditional — it is paid only if at least one unit of the underlying product sells before maturity. If zero units sell, you receive your principal minus a 1% fee, not the advertised return.

2. Business & Market Risk

Opportunities are tied to real small businesses and real product sales. Sales performance can vary and is outside the Platform's control. A business making an overall loss does not, under the current stated model, reduce the advertised investor return — but this places the loss risk on the opportunity owner, which is a significant commercial arrangement that should be reviewed independently.

3. Liquidity Risk

Once an opportunity's investment period starts, your funds are locked until maturity. Early exit is only available before the investment period starts, and carries a 2% fee.

4. Platform & Operational Risk

Hunny Invests currently operates without a dedicated backend server — deposit verification, withdrawal processing, and maturity settlement are performed by admin-controlled workflows rather than automated systems. This introduces manual-process risk (e.g. delays) that a future backend-supported version aims to reduce.

5. No Guaranteed Physical Stock Ownership

Your proportional "stock share" is a participation record for transparency purposes. It does not currently guarantee direct physical ownership or delivery of stock; the final settlement mechanism (cash vs. physical) will be clarified before any large-scale real-money launch.

6. Regulatory Status

This Platform's legal and regulatory structure in Pakistan has not yet been fully established. Do not treat any content on this Platform as investment advice, a licensed financial product, or a legally guaranteed instrument.

7. Your Responsibility

Only invest what you can afford to have locked until maturity and can afford to risk. Read each opportunity's terms carefully before investing.
